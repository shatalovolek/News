AI brief: written by Claude (in Claude Code, on request) from the headlines, article texts, filings and StockTwits data shown on this page; it reports, it does not advise. SEC filings and insider transactions from EDGAR (official, 90 days; open-market buys and sales only in the totals). Relative performance: daily closes from Yahoo Finance, sector ETF chosen by industry. Calendar: earnings dates from Finnhub when configured (confirmed, with consensus estimates), otherwise StockAnalysis estimates. Analysts: Finnhub recommendation trends (monthly counts) and earnings calendar; price targets are not fetched. Social: StockTwits public stream (bullish/bearish tags are set by the posters), Google Trends (relative search interest, 100 = peak of the period). Fundamentals: Finviz or StockAnalysis, refreshed with the news; trailing-twelve-month figures where applicable. Sources: Google News and Yahoo Finance RSS, plus Tavily and Finnhub company news when configured, de-duplicated, collected several times a day for every tracked company. "Most important" ranks headlines by a heuristic: real events (earnings, deals, lawsuits, launches) from major outlets score high; holdings filings, listicles and stock-tip pieces score low. Tone tags come from a keyword heuristic on the headline and are a reading aid, not investment advice. Times are shown in your local time zone.
